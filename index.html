Questions

What will it fit?

The clamp opens wide enough for most patio chair arms, deck railings and table edges, like the outdoor dining chair in the photos. The hand holds a standard can.

How long until I get it?

Each one is printed after you order. You'll get an email from Stripe right away, and we'll follow up when yours is printed and on its way.

Can I pick it up instead?

Yes. Choose local pickup at checkout and we'll reach out to set a time.

Can it stay outside?

It's fine outdoors through normal use and rain. Like most 3D-printed plastic, it can soften in extreme heat, so don't leave it on a car dashboard in summer.

Are the colors exact?

The color photos show how each filament looks on the same hand. Real filament can look slightly different in person. Rainbow is a color-shifting filament, so the blend lands a little differently on every print.
